# Product Vision

## What Is Artha?

**Artha** (Sanskrit: wealth, purpose, material well-being) is a personal finance platform designed to help individuals understand, manage, and grow their financial life with clarity and confidence.

Artha is not a trading platform or a get-rich-quick tool. It is a thoughtful system for tracking money, setting goals, and making informed financial decisions.

## Problem Statement

Most people lack a single, trustworthy place to:

- See a complete picture of their finances
- Understand spending patterns over time
- Set and track financial goals
- Make decisions based on accurate, up-to-date data

Spreadsheets, disconnected apps, and opaque bank interfaces leave users without clarity.

## Target Users

| Segment | Need |
|---|---|
| **Individual savers** | Track income, expenses, and savings in one place |
| **Budget-conscious households** | Categorize spending and stay within limits |
| **Goal-oriented planners** | Monitor progress toward specific financial milestones |

Initial release focuses on individual users. Multi-user and household features are planned for later phases.

## Product Goals

1. **Clarity** — Present financial data in a way that is easy to understand at a glance.
2. **Accuracy** — Ensure numbers are reliable and traceable to their source.
3. **Simplicity** — Reduce friction in daily financial tracking.
4. **Trust** — Handle sensitive financial data with security and privacy as first-class concerns.

## Non-Goals (Initial Phases)

- Investment trading or brokerage integration
- Tax filing or legal financial advice
- Cryptocurrency wallet management
- Social or community features

These may be revisited in future roadmap phases with explicit product approval.

## Success Metrics (Future)

- User can create an account and log in securely
- User can record transactions and view balances
- User can set a financial goal and track progress
- Core workflows complete in under three clicks

## Alignment with Engineering

Product vision informs architecture but does not dictate implementation details. Engineers implement features through approved tickets that reference this document and `05_Development_Roadmap.md`.
