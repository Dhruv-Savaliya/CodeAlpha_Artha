# Product Vision

## What Is Artha?

**Artha** is a premium, AI-powered coffee e-commerce platform designed to craft a luxurious, minimalistic, and cinematic online shopping experience.

Artha is not just a digital storefront or a simple utility for buying beans. It is a storytelling platform, an interactive brand experience, and a personalized sensory journey that builds an emotional connection with coffee lovers and premium lifestyle consumers.

## Problem Statement

Most e-commerce platforms treat specialty coffee as a commodity, resulting in:

- Lacking brand storytelling and emotional resonance
- Generic interfaces that fail to represent the craftsmanship behind the coffee
- Overwhelming, unpersonalized catalogs with no sensory guidance
- Impersonal subscription models that feel transactional rather than curated

Artha replaces this utility-focused shopping experience with an elegant, immersive journey.

## Target Users

| Segment | Need |
|---|---|
| **Coffee Connoisseurs** | Discover rare, single-origin micro-lots with detailed origin storytelling |
| **Premium Lifestyle Customers** | Experience a luxurious, aesthetic, and highly polished shopping process |
| **Gift Buyers** | Find curated specialty sets and premium packaging options with ease |
| **Subscription Customers** | Enjoy a highly personalized, automated curation of freshly roasted coffee |

## Product Goals

1. **Luxurious & Minimalistic** — Impart a sense of craftsmanship and exclusivity through a quiet, breathing visual design.
2. **Immersive Storytelling** — Integrate rich media, motion design, and origin narratives directly into the purchasing flow.
3. **Personalization** — Utilize AI to offer sensory-based product recommendations matched to the user's taste profile.
4. **Emotional Subscription** — Design a replenishment flow that feels like a premium club membership rather than a utility bill.

## Non-Goals (Initial Phases)

- B2B or wholesale distribution portals
- Physical café point-of-sale (POS) integration
- Third-party marketplace hosting
- International shipping customs management

These may be revisited in future roadmap phases with explicit product approval.

## Success Metrics (Future)

- User can browse catalogs and view cinematic product pages
- User can customize and checkout a subscription or single order
- User can receive personalized recommendations based on a quick taste quiz
- Core checkout workflows complete in under three steps

## Alignment with Engineering

Product vision informs architecture but does not dictate implementation details. Engineers implement features through approved tickets that reference this document and `05_Development_Roadmap.md`.
